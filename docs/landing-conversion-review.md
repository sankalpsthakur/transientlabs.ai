# Landing review and release —13October2026

Goal: qualified project enquiries. Secondary path: readers/listeners exploring the engineering before contacting the studio. No traffic, conversion baseline or visitor research was available for this change; these are design hypotheses, not a measured uplift.

## Quick wins implemented

- The landing previously had no podcast path. Header/mobile menu, hero, latest-work feature and footer now link directly to `/the-deep-end`.
- The hero now describes a working AI product/workflow and the engineering handoff instead of promising margin gains without a supporting result. One primary action, “Scope my project”, opens the existing enquiry form.
- Scope and fees are explained before the visitor acts; the modal makes company optional and describes the response, rather than implying an instantly booked calendar slot.
- The large university/employer logo block was removed from the first screen. Existing collaborator assets and the established brand remain; no new affiliation or testimonial is claimed.
- Tablet navigation uses the same usable menu as mobile instead of squeezing desktop links into a narrow header. Sticky mobile CTA events use the existing analytics utility.

## Evidence and high-impact changes

Recent work now appears before the older product-interface showcase and industry list. The podcast feature derives its episode/companion counts from the catalogue on the server, passing only a small summary to the landing client. The initial release contains96 approved episode pages and79 verified interactive companions. Seventeen remaining pages have covers/notes/listening links without false interactive badges.

The public repositories [gridload-compact](https://github.com/sankalpsthakur/gridload-compact) and [3W held-out-well](https://github.com/sankalpsthakur/3w-held-out-well) were freshly read for project scope. The landing links to implementation/evaluation evidence and states their research limits. It claims no official rank, customer deployment, measured business gain or research breakthrough. The older showcase is described as product interfaces rather than independently verified recent production builds.

The founder explicitly authorized immediate website release, superseding the earlier wait for universal podcast propagation. This staged release does not complete all96 visuals or universal directory propagation. Existing routes, GUIDs, audio, dedicated covers, creator links and Claude Opus/Edge TTS disclosure are preserved.

## Test ideas and copy alternatives

Measure `cta_click → form_start → generate_lead`, segmented by device/source, alongside podcast navigation. Contact delivery and form submissions are separate from modal-open evidence. The release checks do not send synthetic leads to the team.

- Test “Scope my project” against “Book a working session”; evaluate qualified enquiries, not just clicks.
- Test the current “Ship AI systems your team can rely on” against “Turn your manual workflow into a working AI system” for workflow-specific traffic. Use “A senior team for your next AI product” for founder traffic; do not switch messaging without traffic context.
- Compare the recent-work feature near the top against the product-interface showcase first. Keep the primary commercial CTA available in both variants.

Retain the current brand identity. Adding pricing guarantees, response commitments, customer metrics, affiliations or testimonials requires verified business evidence. “Highly converting” remains an outcome to measure after deployment, not a claim established by a build or screenshot.
