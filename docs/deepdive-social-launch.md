## Suggested launch copy

### Combined reel

The AI economy is physical before it is digital.

Orbit supplies the network. Data-center campuses turn power into intelligence. Firm generation sets the capacity boundary.

The Physical Stack is an interactive systems deep dive from Transient Labs—now with enterprise decision briefs that turn technical spectacle into operating models, diligence questions, and executable artifacts.

Explore: https://stack.transientlabs.ai

### Satellites

The network is becoming a moving shell of routers in low Earth orbit. The enterprise question is not “how many satellites?” It is where density, spectrum, gateways, terminal economics, and replenishment create a defensible service.

Deep dive: https://stack.transientlabs.ai/satellites

### Data centers

An AI campus is a power and thermal system that happens to compute. The binding constraint may be interconnection, cooling, fiber, permits, or commissioning sequence—long before it is GPUs.

Deep dive: https://stack.transientlabs.ai/data-centers

### Nuclear / SMR

Firm power is not a capacity placeholder. It is a program of load, site, licensing, fuel, vendors, construction sequence, and operating authority.

Deep dive: https://stack.transientlabs.ai/nuclear

## Publishing checklist

- Use the combined reel for the launch post and the standalone cuts as follow-ups.
- Upload the MP4 directly to each platform; do not post an external video link as the primary media.
- Keep the first two lines above the fold and place the deep-dive URL after the core argument.
- Use each matching poster as the thumbnail where the platform permits one.
- Verify `stack.transientlabs.ai` resolves before publishing the URLs.
