# Episode81 — Karatsuba recombiner

Concept and parts planned before geometry. Figure, one mechanism, about65–75 true supported solids;30–90minutes. An invented three-product desk calculator has three matched calibrated product cartridges, a four-digit carry window and four knurled decimal input controls. Its high, low and sum cartridges replace the four pairwise products of a two-digit schoolbook split. Selecting a stage lights its cartridge and explains what is being reconstructed; the same exact integer model drives needles, output digits and native controls.

Parts by function: rounded bench; four pad/post feet; four corner fasteners; three product flange/body/face/hub cartridges with four screws each; two retaining shaft/roller/cap stacks; two carry-window supports, bed and four retaining rims with eight screws; four input seat/shaft/grip stacks; nameplate with two fasteners. Every part has a real flush host. Needles, stage lamps and numerical display are scientific/status overlays, not pretend rigid solids. Measured true-shape painter order, no guessed key biases. Fixed dial range0–324 shared by all three cartridges, no autoscaling.

Established decimal split X=10hX+lX,Y=10hY+lY, four native digits0…9. H=hX*hY,L=lX*lY,S=(hX+lX)*(hY+lY),M=S−H−L. Reconstruct XY=100H+10M+L. Carry units from L, then carry from M plus that carry; the high block can have two digits. Inputs have two places even when leading zero; output is four zero-padded decimal digits. Gauge maxima H/L81,S324; common324 scale explicitly labelled. Four native stage values high/low/sum/assemble. All products are computed and inspectable in every stage; manual sequence is an educational highlight, not a runtime clock or computation benchmark. Manual Run stops at assembly without looping. Reduced motion leaves still digit/stage controls.

The example12×34 gives H3,L8,S21,M10,result408.99×99 exposes both carry steps and9801. Four chunk products versus three is not a25% latency/bit-operation saving: sums can have an extra digit and recombination has additions/subtractions/carries. This figure does not implement a full recursive multiplier, FFT, new asymptotic bound, changed computation model, exact-DFT research claim or paper/Lean fidelity audit.
