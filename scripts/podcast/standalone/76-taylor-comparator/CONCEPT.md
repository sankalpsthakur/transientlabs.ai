# Episode76 — Taylor comparator

Figure concept and parts list written12October2026 before geometry. Two matched profile cartridges and two error dials compare what local Taylor data reconstructs: an analytic Gaussian and a smooth compact bump. The probe moves along both fixed profiles; the two dials show the same absolute-discrepancy scale. About76 purposeful solids and60–90minutes. This isolates the smooth-versus-analytic hinge in the approved episode, without testing the CIV conditional PDE theorem or any paper/Lean claim.

Parts: rounded base, four footpads/posts, four corner screws; two cartridge pairs of feet, two beds, eight perimeter rails, sixteen rail screws; each cartridge has two spindle bases/rollers/caps; two gauge flanges/bodies/faces/spindles and four retention screws; two control seats/shafts/knurled grips; nameplate/two screws. Every true slab/cylinder sits flush on its support. Scientific traces and probe dots are model overlays, not mechanical cables. Measured true-solid depth order, no hand bias.

Exact normalized model: B(x)=exp(1−1/(1−x²)) for |x|<1, and0 otherwise. All derivatives of B at the left boundary x=−1 vanish, so every Taylor polynomial there is zero. It nevertheless peaks at B(0)=1. G(x)=exp(−(x+1)²) is analytic. Its Taylor polynomial of degree2N about−1 is T_N(x)=sum_{j=0}^N (−1)^j(x+1)^(2j)/j!. N=0,1,2,4,6,10,14. Probe/window x∈[−1.5,.5]; it deliberately shows the left boundary, not the whole bump support. Both cartridges use fixed vertical range[−1.5,1.5], both error dials[0,1.5]. No autoscaling. Analytic discrepancy is |G−T_N|; smooth discrepancy is |B−0|. Finite-N Gaussian error is approximation error, while the bump mismatch persists at every order. At x=−1 the point data match exactly; inside the bump a zero infinite Taylor series does not determine the function. This is an explicit example, not a general derivative-estimation or numerical certification tool.

Controls: native degree selector, native probe slider. Presets boundary, near boundary, bump peak, coarse Gaussian. Manual sweep stops at.5, reduced motion uses still frames.
