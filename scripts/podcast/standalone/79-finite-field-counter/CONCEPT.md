# Episode79 — Finite-field point counter

Concept and parts planned before geometry. An invented residue-inspection bench has one wide gridded sighting window, an infinity-point station, two calibrated count/difference dials and three knurled coefficient/cursor controls. The sighting window marks exact modular solutions and highlights the selected x column. Discrete points and dial needles are scientific overlays, not961 pretend rigid solids. Target60–80 true supported rounded solids, measured depth order and structural stroke checks;60–90minutes including model/browser checks.

Parts: rounded bench; four feet/post pairs and four corner screws; two window supports, one large bed and four rim strips with twelve fasteners; four retaining roller seat/body/cap stacks; two dial flange/body/face/spindle stacks and four screws; three seat/shaft/grip stacks; infinity-point plinth/holder/face and two screws; nameplate and two screws. Each is a true flush supported shape; no painter bias. Static geometry, manual x sweep only. The finite residue grid is labelled0…p−1 in each axis and changes spacing with p, not a real continuous curve.

Established short-Weierstrass family y²=x³+ax+b modulo odd prime p>3, p5/7/11/13/17/19/23/29/31. Native integers a,b,x0…p−1. Count every affine pair and add the unique projective point O=[0:1:0]. Δ=−16(4a³+27b²) mod p; only Δ≠0 makes this an elliptic curve and gives the Frobenius trace t=p+1−N with |t|≤2√p. The count dial is fixed0–64; normalized trace dial fixed−1…1 for t/(2√p). Singular cases still count solutions and O, but trace/Hasse comparison is explicitly unavailable and the muted dial parks at centre without assigning zero.

Selected column reports the cubic residue and0/1/2 square roots. y0 is one root; nonzero roots pair y,p−y. Changing p clamps a,b,x and writes max before value. Presets include two-root, one-root, no-root and singular examples. Sweeps stop at p−1, no wrapping or idle animation. Reduced motion keeps still controls.

This is finite arithmetic, not a computation of rational rank, analytic continuation, L(E,1), Sha, BSD, Hodge classes, complex geometry or paper/Lean fidelity. It does not validate episode research claims.
