# Episode 80 — Blow-up direction resolver

Concept and parts list written before geometry, 13 October 2026. Figure, one mechanism, about 60–75 true supported solids; 30–90 minutes. An invented optical survey bench pairs a square position plate with a circular direction register. Contracting a point toward the origin makes the position marker converge, while the direction register retains which line it came from. The dials, markers and native controls share one exact incidence model.

Parts grouped by function: bench plate; four pad/post feet; four corner screws; two position-window supports; square window bed; four retaining rim strips; twelve rim screws; circular direction flange/body/face/hub; six flange screws; two three-part retaining rollers; two gauge flange/body/face/spindle stacks with four flange screws; two control seat/shaft/knurled grip stacks; nameplate and two fasteners. Everything rests flush on a real host. Grid lines, position/direction markers, guide line and dial needles are scientific overlays, not imaginary rigid solids. Static geometry with measured true-shape painter order.

Model: the real points of the established affine-plane blow-up B = {((x,y),[u:v]): xv = yu}. Controls use signed r in [-1,1] (step .01) and theta in [0,180] degrees (step .5), with u=cos(theta), v=sin(theta), x=r*u, y=r*v. At r=0, the base point is (0,0), but the projective direction remains. Away from the origin the direction is uniquely [x:y]. Two charts: u nonzero gives (x,t=v/u) and y=x*t; v nonzero gives (s=u/v,y) and x=y*s. Unavailable denominators at exact horizontal/vertical cases yield N/A, never infinity or a fabricated zero. Chart determinants x and y illustrate the contraction; numerical floating residuals are diagnostics only.

The circular direction register is an explicitly labelled schematic of RP1 using angle 2*theta, so 0 and 180 are the same line. This is not the complex projective line as a circle. The coordinate presentation is twisted: (r,180) and (-r,0) represent the same incidence point. Signed r is not an invariant distance; |r| is the Euclidean distance in this real teaching slice. Fixed base window [-1,1] in both coordinates; fixed signed-r gauge [-1,1]. No autoscaling. Manual contraction stops at zero without looping; still controls remain under reduced motion.
