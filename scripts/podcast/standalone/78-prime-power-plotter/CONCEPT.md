# Episode78 — Prime-power plotter

Figure concept written before geometry. A matched two-cartridge bench totalizer plots the prime-power staircase and compares a measured finite integer residual with a chosen power ruler. The two fixed dials read the selected weighted count divided by the selected range limit, and the largest normalized residual across the visible integer range. Two knurled controls mark cursor and exponent; four rollers retain the recording cartridges. About76 purposeful true solids;60–90minutes including verification.
