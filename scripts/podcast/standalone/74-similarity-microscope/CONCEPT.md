# Episode74 — Similarity microscope

Figure concept and parts list written12October2026 before geometry. An invented line-scan microscope shows a shrinking profile on its observation strip; fixed mesh samples miss the centre when they fall between it, while a normalized eyepiece view keeps the same profile. Two fixed-scale dials compare true and sampled central axial vorticity. The microscopic family is illustrative, not a solution of Euler or Navier–Stokes. About60–70 purposeful solids,60–90minutes.

Parts: rounded base plate, four footpads/posts, four mounting screws, engraved nameplate/two screws; two stage feet, long observation bed, viewing inlay, four perimeter rails, eight retaining screws; two rear bridge feet/posts/caps, crossbar/four screws; optical collar, barrel and lower lens tip; two meter flanges/bodies/faces/spindles/four screws; time and mesh-phase knob seats/shafts/knurled grips; rescaled-view seat, cylinder, face and ring-retaining screws. Every solid is flush on its support. The profile, grid and samples are scientific overlays, not physical wires. Trace and sampling marks are clipped to the window plane. No arrows/labels float in the drawing.

Pure model: U(s)=(-s_y,s_x,0) exp(-|s|²), a smooth divergence-free3D Gaussian swirl. u_tau(x)=tau^(-alpha) U(x/tau^beta), positive remaining scale tau in[.125,1], alpha in[0,1], beta in[.25,1]. This is a kinematic family that has not been shown to satisfy a fluid momentum equation. Length ell=tau^beta, central axial curl2 tau^(-alpha-beta), and whole-space squared-L2 energy ratio tau^(3beta-2alpha). Base integral is(1/2)(pi/2)^(3/2). Along the x-axis, omega_z(x)=2 tau^(-alpha-beta)(1-(x/ell)²) exp(-(x/ell)²). The normalized eyepiece plots omega_z(ell s)/omega_z(0)=(1-s²)exp(-s²), which stays unchanged. The full-scale strip uses the same relative-height normalization; fixed dials carry absolute values0–128.

Mesh: N equal intervals across[-3,3], N8,16,32,64. Shift samples by phase in[0,.5] of h=6/N and retain positions inside the window. The positive sampled peak is max(0,omega_z(x_j)); the fractional underestimate is1-sampled/central. Probe spheres are drawn at those exact x_j and normalized field heights. Nearest-centre marker highlights the sampling offset. These are exact evaluations at a finite grid, not a discretized PDE solver or a rigorous bound. Changing the mesh phase never moves the underlying profile.

Presets: coarse and refined usealpha=.5,beta=.75 withN8/64 andhalf-cell phase; finite-energy scaling usesalpha=1,beta=1; rising energy usesalpha=1,beta=.25. Time starts tau=.5 and manualPlay reduces it only to.125, then stops; no infinity is drawn and no finite-element tolerance certificate is claimed. Near tau0 this invented scaling would diverge, but that does not establish a fluid singularity or a smooth force. No adaptive-solver, neural-network, Lean, current2026research or theorem claims are reproduced as verified.
