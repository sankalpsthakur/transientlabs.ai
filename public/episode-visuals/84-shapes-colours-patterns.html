How is the polar constructed?

The polar is K°={y: x·y≤1 for every x in K}. For this bounded planar family, K(t)={|x|≤1, |y|≤1, |x|+|y|≤2−t}. Its polar joins axis points (±1,0), (0,±1) and diagonal points (±a,±a), a=1/(2−t). Redundant points disappear at the square and diamond endpoints.

Before stretching, areas are A=4−2t² and B=4/(2−t). Horizontal stretch T=diag(λ,1) gives areas λA and B/λ, keeping product AB unchanged. The direction n=(cosθ,sinθ) has support h=max(n·v); the polar point n/h satisfies x·(n/h)≤1, with equality at every highlighted vertex.
