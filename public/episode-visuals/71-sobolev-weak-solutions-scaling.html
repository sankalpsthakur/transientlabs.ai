What this instrument measures

The unforced whole-space Navier–Stokes rescaling is uλ(x,t)=λu(λx,λ²t). Length scales by1/λ, velocity byλ and time by1/λ². All readings are ratios to a normalized baseline, not equipment units. The caliper is an invented analogue for characteristic length; no fluid simulation is running.

Energy ratio: λ²⁻ᵈ
Homogeneous Ḣˢ ratio: λ¹⁺ˢ⁻ᵈ⁄²

Energy is a squared L2 norm, while the other rulers display norms or seminorms. The critical order is s=d/2−1: half a derivative in3D and zero derivatives in2D. Energy falls like1/λ in3D, but stays constant in2D. The H1 option is the homogeneous gradient seminorm, not the full inhomogeneous H1 norm.

These exact identities require whole-space profiles with the indicated finite homogeneous norms. A fixed bounded domain or a full inhomogeneous Sobolev norm has different bookkeeping. The dimension selector changes the mathematical domain; the illustrated bench remains a three-dimensional object. Scaling alone proves neither singularity nor regularity, and the comparator does not test an existence theorem.
