What this model shows

Each pin samples a dimensionless field on a periodic one-metre domain. The highlighted cap and the front dial read the same sample. No heat enters or leaves; the right boundary wraps to the left.

uₜ + v uₓ = κ uₓₓ

The exact solution combines two cosine modes. Velocity moves them; diffusivity damps the shorter mode faster. This linear teaching model does not represent shocks, turbulent flow or singularity claims.
