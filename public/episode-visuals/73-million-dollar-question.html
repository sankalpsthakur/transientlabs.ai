What this viewer reveals

Gaussian axial vorticity under fixed strain u_r=−ar/2, u_z=az. Initially w=8 mm; Γ=1000 mm²/s stays fixed. The contour marks 1/e of the peak.

ω(r,t)=Γ exp(−r²/q)/(πq)
q=w² · q′=4ν−aq

Substitution into the vorticity equation gives this width law. For a>0, steady radius is √(4ν/a). At a=0, q=64+4νt with no steady radius. Fixed dials: 0–20 mm and 0–350 s⁻¹.

The 24 mm window clips the infinite tail. Shading is relative to the current peak; brightness cannot compare absolute values between settings. The sample and circulation reading use this same field.

This cross-section uses imposed strain, whose velocity grows unboundedly at infinity. The infinite-energy family is outside Clay's finite-energy problem. Controls select trajectories from the same initial profile, without simulating a switched apparatus. No turbulence, instability, full3D solver or general regularity proof is supplied.
